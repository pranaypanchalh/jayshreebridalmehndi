About Me

Hello, I'm Jayshree Panchal, a Professional Celebrity Bridal Mehndi Artist and Trainer based in Nikol, Ahmedabad.

For over three decades, I have been dedicated to the beautiful art of mehndi, transforming traditional henna into timeless masterpieces. Since beginning my journey in 1992, I have had the privilege of decorating thousands of brides' hands with intricate designs that celebrate love, culture, and tradition. Every design I create is handcrafted with passion, precision, and creativity, making each bridal mehndi a unique work of art.
